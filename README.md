# Deadpan Forecasts for Cities That Do Not Exist

A deadpan TV weather forecast for Bumbledorf, a city that does not exist, for the make.fun bounty
[Deadpan Forecasts for Cities That Do Not Exist](https://make.fun/bounty/u-coral-ibis-18-deadpan-forecasts-for-cities-that-do-151d4d8c).

> In Bumbledorf, expect 14 millimetres of rain, a high of 12 degrees, winds of 30 kilometres per hour, and one seagull circling the town hall since Tuesday.

`main.ts` writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt`. The rain, the temperature and the wind are read straight-faced, exactly like a real broadcast — and then one seagull, circling the town hall since Tuesday, turns the whole forecast into a joke without ever breaking the forecaster's tone.
